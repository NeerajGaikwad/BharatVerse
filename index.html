WHY BHARATVERSE EXISTS

Memory is fragile.
Access shouldn't be.

Time, weather, distance, and restricted access can separate people from the heritage around them. BharatVerse began as an SIH project with one simple belief: digital tools can help more people encounter India's art and architecture, wherever they are.

By presenting 3D heritage alongside thoughtful context, we hope to support learning, spark curiosity, and make preservation feel personal. Digital access complements the real places and communities; it never replaces them.

FROM STONE TO SCREEN

Preservation is a practice.

A good digital archive does more than show an object. It helps people understand where it belongs and why it matters.

01
⌕

Document

Capture an object's shape and surface through careful photography, scanning, or a credited open model.

02
◉

Make it explorable

Use an interactive 3D viewer so visitors can rotate, zoom, and notice details at their own pace.

03
✳

Share its story

Add context, provenance, and clear credits—so access comes with respect for people and place.
